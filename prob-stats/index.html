Probability & Statistics Foundations

A six-chapter prerequisite path from probability spaces and distributions to the CLT, classical inference, and regression—built for later work in quantitative finance and machine learning.

Recommended use: read in order if probability and statistics feel fragmented. Each chapter combines intuition, formal theory, worked examples, practical failure modes, and exercises with solution outlines.

The shorter probability and statistics lessons remain useful as quick references; this course supplies the connective tissue.

Chapter 1 — Probability Language

events · random variables · PMF · PDF · CDF

Outcomes and events, probability axioms, discrete versus continuous variables, mixed distributions, transformations, and quantiles.
