Applied Machine Learning

A six-chapter path from statistical learning and regularized linear models to model families, honest evaluation, leakage-resistant validation, and production diagnosis.

Prerequisites: Probability & Statistics Foundations and working linear algebra. The course emphasizes why methods work, what they assume, and how they fail.

Chapter 1 — Learning, Risk & Generalization

risk · bias–variance · capacity · shift

Population versus empirical risk, sources of error, inductive bias, learning theory intuition, and deployment-aware validation.

Chapter 2 — Linear Models, Likelihood & Regularization

Projection, likelihood-derived losses, regularization geometry, optimization, collinearity, and coefficient interpretation.

Chapter 3 — Model Families & Inductive Bias

kNN · trees · ensembles · margins · kernels

Chapter 4 — Evaluation, Calibration & Decisions

metrics · thresholds · ranking · calibration

cross-validation · leakage · features · shift

error analysis · monitoring · feedback · governance
