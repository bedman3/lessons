ML Research Bridge

A practical course for experienced engineers moving into hands-on machine-learning research: define a target, set honest baselines, validate chronologically, and judge evidence carefully.

Prerequisites: basic Python and familiarity with data analysis. The lab uses synthetic data and makes no trading or P&L claims.
